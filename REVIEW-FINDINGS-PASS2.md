**R1-M11 `references/m-6-backups-and-recovery.md`**

*Problem.* Destructive-event runbook step 4 (line 324) offers, as ways to break an attacker's deletion path mid-incident, "on AWS attach a service control policy at the organisational unit denying rds:DeleteDBSnapshot, backup:DeleteRecoveryPoint, s3:DeleteObjectVersion, and kms:ScheduleKeyDeletion" and "on Azure apply a delete lock to the relevant resource group".
